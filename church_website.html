Welcome to God's House

We are a vibrant, faith-filled community in Lusaka, Zambia, dedicated to sharing the uncommon love of God. We believe in the transforming power of the Word and are committed to helping you experience extraordinary results in every area of your life. Come and grow with us!

Our Mission

To make the uncommon love of God common through the preaching and doing of the Word of God.

Our Vision

To raise men and women who are rooted in Christ and equipped to influence their world with the kingdom lifestyle.

Our Core Values

Word-Centered, Love-Driven, Community-Focused, and Excellence in Service.

Our Ministries

Youth Ministry

Empowering the next generation to live boldly for Christ and lead with purpose in a changing world.

Upcoming Event: Youth Meeting after the second service today!

Women's Ministry

A supportive sisterhood dedicated to spiritual growth, mentorship, and impactful community outreach.

Men's Ministry

Challenging men to become spiritual leaders in their homes, church, and marketplace.

Transformers Class

Discipleship training for new members to equip them with foundational truths and prepare them for ministry and Christian living.
