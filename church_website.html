Welcome to God's House

We are a vibrant, faith-filled community in Lusaka, Zambia, dedicated to sharing the uncommon love of God. We believe in the transforming power of the Word and are committed to helping you experience extraordinary results in every area of your life. Come and grow with us!

Our Mission

To make the uncommon love of God common through the preaching and doing of the Word of God.

Our Vision

To raise men and women who are rooted in Christ and equipped to influence their world with the kingdom lifestyle.

Our Core Values

Word-Centered, Love-Driven, Community-Focused, and Excellence in Service.
